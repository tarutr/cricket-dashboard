`review/FEATURE_LEDGER.md`, checked in beside the decision log, as the durable source of truth for how the
built app conforms to every owner ruling. It began as a Phase-0.1 working copy under `.orchestrator/`; that

alongside the sweep gates in `.orchestrator/_gates/`).

**Purpose.** One row per owner ruling that has OBSERVABLE behaviour, converted from Audit 1's
checkpoint-by-checkpoint findings (`.orchestrator/progress/audit1-rulings.md`) plus the 2026-08-24 rulings
recorded in `stage3-fix-plan.md`'s "Key rulings" block. Every row traces to a specific sentence in one of
those two documents, or (for a handful of rows carrying direct wording) `review/owner_decisions.md` itself.
Nothing here is invented or independently re-investigated — statuses are exactly what the audits found, not
a fresh code read. Where `audit2-popup.md` or `audit3-autocols-defects.md` added live-verified precision to
a finding Audit 1 already flagged, that detail is folded into the row's Notes (their content is never the
sole source of a row — see the Checkpoint coverage map at the bottom).

**How to read "Status today"** (vocabulary fixed to these five values; a row's Status cell always LEADS with
one of them, with any nuance after an em dash or in Notes):
- **CONFORMS** — built and matches the ruling (a superset or a harmless label drift still counts as CONFORMS,
  flagged in Notes).
- **GAP** — never worked / never built, or built only partially, with no owner sign-off to leave it that way.
- **LOST** — worked once (there is commit evidence), then silently regressed with no ratifying decision on
  record.
- **DEFERRED** — explicitly parked by the owner or the approved plan; the row says where/when it is due.
- **NEEDS-HUMAN** — the audits found a genuine open question, a live-behaviour question no static check can
  settle, or two sources in tension — an owner ruling or a live look is needed before this can be scored.
